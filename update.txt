ArenaLab update v1
version: 1.4.0
note: Nameplates and locators: make your own text animation. With Edit on, the Animation timeline shows four needles -- fade-in starts, fade-in ends, fade-out starts, fade-out ends. Drag them and the preview follows while you drag. The game's animation file is never touched: when you save, ArenaLab writes a copy next to your arena (UI/Assets/show_animations/<family>/<project>_Nameplate_Anim.jsfb, or _Locator_Anim) with your timing, and the project points at it. Pack it with the arena. Undo works like any other change. It works with the usual fade-in / fade-out animations (102 of the game's 103 nameplate animations and 91 of its 104 locator ones); animations from another pack's file have to be swapped for a game one first.
note: Nameplates and locators: if your timing ends up within half a second of a game animation on all four needles, a note above the timeline offers "Use the game one" or "Keep mine". Using the game one means no extra animation file in your .cak.
note: ShowAssets: new camera button above the canvas that exports the preview as a 1920x1080 PNG, exactly what you are looking at but without the frame, selection boxes, layer names or the placeholders for missing pieces. With a nameplate or locator animation it exports the frame on screen. It remembers the last folder, and never suggests the arena's own folder, so the image does not end up packed into the .cak.
note: ShowAssets: group layers such as FontRoot, which draw nothing themselves, now have a box on the canvas: the area their content covers. You can click them, select them and drag them with everything inside.
note: ShowAssets: the selected layer shows its origin as a cross, with a line to its box when the origin sits outside it. That is how a ported nameplate hides a name that is placed right in the preview and off to one side in game.
note: ShowAssets: new "Origin to centre on X" / "on Y" for layers that contain others, and the origin cross can be dragged on the canvas. The origin moves and every child is compensated, so nothing on screen shifts by a pixel.
note: ShowAssets: a text with a zoom other than 1 is now drawn shifted to the side the way the game draws it, so ported nameplates that carry a zoom no longer look centred here and off-centre in game.
note: ArenaDef: the four PropsSet slots are named -- Exhibition, Career, Hell in a Cell and Underground -- with a note on what each one does in the game's arenas. Hell in a Cell is the one that removes the championship pedestal and belts. Party Mode (Backstage and NXT Parking Lot only) and The Archives' unnamed slot are shown too, and are not aligned with the other four.
