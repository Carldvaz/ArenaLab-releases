ArenaLab update v1
version: 1.3.0
kind: hotfix
note: ShowAssets: fixed matchups that came out completely black in game. "Use this font in the file" with a font from ArenaLab's own font folder wrote just its file name (like font_match.fntpck), a path the game cannot find. Now the font is copied into your arena (UI/Assets/show_fonts for .fntpck, UI/Assets/Slug/ShowFonts/Fonts for .slug) and the project points there, so pack it with the arena. If one of your matchups went black, open it, pick its font again and press "Use this font in the file".
note: Fonts: new "Make a .slug from a typeface…" in the font editor. Pick a typeface installed on your PC or a .ttf/.otf file and export: the conversion runs in the background with nothing to set. It needs one game .slug extracted to use as a base, and it asks before saving over the path of a game font, which would change it in every matchup. Important: to pack a .slug made with ArenaLab into a .cak you need the upcoming CakeView update. With the current CakeView the text can come out blank in game.
note: Fonts: a shadow at (0,0) now shows as an outline with fonts made in ArenaLab. The game draws shadows with a slightly thicker copy of each letter, and the converter was making that copy the same size, so the shadow hid behind the text. Fonts you already made need exporting again.
note: ShowAssets: matchup texts now draw with the real letter shapes of their .slug font, even when that typeface is not installed on your PC, gradient and shadow included.
note: Fonts: game fonts you have not extracted now show in the font picker with their real name (Proxima Nova Black, Futura Bold…). The app carries their character widths and line metrics, so line breaks and the "does not fit" warning are the game's even when the preview uses a stand-in typeface. Picking one offers to select the extracted file or your game fonts folder, or to preview with a typeface from your PC. The letters themselves are not shipped: they belong to their foundries.
note: Settings: new optional "Game fonts" folder. Point it at a folder with the extracted UI/Assets/Slug and UI/Assets/show_fonts and every game font is available without opening the base files.
note: Fonts: fixed the .slug reader. Characters such as ! " # ¡ ¢ £ were missing, a few letters came out as other ones (Ċ as Č, Σ as T) and the space had a made-up width. Line widths in the preview are closer to the game now.
note: ShowAssets: if the matchup's font is inside your open arena it is picked automatically, installed or not. If it is not among the app's fonts yet, you are asked once whether to add it.
note: ShowAssets: Shift+click selects several layers, on the canvas or in the layer list. Drag them together, change a field for all of them at once (fields that differ show "mixed"), and undo it in one step. X/Y and Center move the group instead of piling it up.
note: ShowAssets: "Copy style" and "Paste style…" (Ctrl+Alt+C / Ctrl+Alt+V). Tick what to paste: position, size, tint, effects, alignment and font. It also works between files, for example from a matchup to its nameplate. If a paste would force the file to be rebuilt instead of saved in place, it warns you right away.
note: ShowAssets: "Select matching layers" picks the same layer (Name1, Match, Belt…) in every scene of a matchup or in the 7 variants of a nameplate. Hold Shift to add them to what you already had.
note: Matchups: the text scenes are named for what they are (1v1 title match, Non-title match, Multi-man title match, Universe promo / Create a Show preview) and the scene list only offers the ones that apply to the chosen line-up. Ticking Title switches the scene. The file still keeps all of them: the game picks one when the match starts.
note: ShowAssets: texts with their own zoom are placed where the game puts them. The game scales their box twice, so a left-aligned name with zoom could look right in the app and run off the screen in game. The selection frame now follows the text's alignment. "Move the zoom into font size and box" fixes them.
note: Nameplates and locators: new Animation panel under the canvas. Play the video with its transparency and see on a coloured timeline when the text fades in, stays and fades out. Choose among the game's 103 nameplate / 104 locator animations or the ones in your open folders, with their names. Only the link to the animation is written, and a red warning shows when the text stays on screen after the video ends.
note: Nameplates: packs that use a different video for each text combination (belt, manager…) play the video of the combination you pick.
note: ShowAssets: a 2K25 matchup, nameplate, locator or result opened in the editor is converted to 2K26 in memory as it opens and marked unsaved. Edits that rebuild the file no longer break a 2K25 matchup.
note: Videos: Bink 2 videos with transparency, and their thumbnails, came out opaque. They keep their transparency now.
note: ArenaData: entrance ramp 4 is identified. It is "Almost flat", between 12 and 6, and sits in its place in the ramp list.
