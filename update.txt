ArenaLab update v1
version: 1.5.0
note: Matchups: custom renders. A new "Who fights" row under the cast lets you type each wrestler's name and pick your own render for each slot, in PNG, TIFF, DDS, TGA, PSD, JPG or WebP, transparency included. It is only for the preview: nothing is written to the file, and your picks stay in the same slots when you open another matchup.
note: ShowAssets: videos are now placed and scaled the way the game does it. A video with its own scale or offset (the Clash at the Castle Paris and SummerSlam 08 nameplates, for example) is drawn at that size and position in the preview instead of always filling the screen, and a video can be stretched on the canvas.
note: Results: a new Result picker (One man / Team / Draw) shows one ending at a time, as the game does, instead of the three names on top of each other. The label above the name now reads WINNER.
